{%- comment -%} Sortable reviews table for /reviews. Built from post front matter at build time, so a new post with `rating:` shows up on the next build. Fields per post (all optional except rating): product Shown in the Product column. Falls back to the title. brand Blank shows a dash and sorts last. price One amount as stated in the post, e.g. "$129" or "€30". Blank shows a dash and sorts last. Never look one up. price_note Short qualifier under the price, e.g. "retail (Nov 2026)". review_channel Channel from the review box when it differs from `channel`. Thumbnails come from post-thumb.html, the same picker the post cards use (resized /images/thumbs/ file, else the post image, else the first content photo). Posts with none get a neutral placeholder box. Rows render newest first without JavaScript. reviews-table.js adds sortable column buttons and aria-sort. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign rt_posts = site.posts | where_exp: "item", "item.rating != nil" -%} {%- assign static_index = site.static_files | map: "path" | join: "||" | prepend: "||" | append: "||" -%}

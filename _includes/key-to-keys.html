Because the Commodore Plus/4 keyboard is so different from modern keyboards, I devised a modern key nomenclature to identify keystroke combinations as shown below:

  • CapsCaps Lock
  • F1Function 1
  • C=Commodore
  • F2Function 2
  • CtrlControl
  • F3Function 3
  • EscEscape
  • F4Function 4
  • Clr/HomeClear/Home
  • F5Function 5
  • Inst/DelInsert Delete
  • F6Function 6
  • ReturnReturn
  • F7Function 7
  • R/SRun/Stop
  • F8Help
  • ShiftShift
  • SpaceSpace
  • ↑Cursor Up
  • ↓Cursor Down
  • →Cursor Right
  • ←Cursor Left

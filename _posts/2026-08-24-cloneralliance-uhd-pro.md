## WHAT'S IN THE BOX {#whats-in-the-box}

The box has the recorder, the remote, a barrel-jack power supply, an HDMI cable, a micro-USB cable, and the full user guide.

## DESIGN AND PORTS {#design-and-ports}

My unit is 4.6 x 4.6 x 1.1 in and weighs 8.5 oz, from manual v1.0.2. The HDMI IN and OUT ports are mounted upside down relative to a typical player. The front has Line In, Line Out, Mic, a USB storage port, and the H.264, Snapshot, and Stop buttons.

![Front panel of the ClonerAlliance UHD Pro with Line In, Line Out, Mic, and the H.264, Snapshot, and Stop buttons](/images/posts/2026-08-24-cloneralliance-uhd-pro/front-panel.jpg)

On my revision the status ring shows the codec. Blue means H.264. Green means H.265. That's the side-indicator table in the original manual.

## POWER AND THE REMOTE {#power-and-remote}

The remote has no power button. The unit uses a push switch, and it takes about 15 seconds to boot.

> **Tip:** A smart plug, including one tied to Home Assistant, gets around the push switch. That's how I power mine. I don't have an affiliate link for a specific plug, so I'm not linking one here.
{: .tip}

## SETUP WITH AN ANDROID TV BOX {#setup}

The path is simple. The source goes into HDMI IN, the TV comes off HDMI OUT, and the drive goes in the front USB port.

> **Note:** Plug the USB drive or TF card in before you power the unit on. If the drive isn't seen, power off, insert it, and boot again. ([Manual FAQ](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/B1MLjXvNAyL.pdf))
{: .note}

## ONE-TOUCH RECORDING: H.264 VS H.265 {#one-touch-recording}

Pick the codec with the front button or the menu. One press starts recording. I didn't see a pause before the file started. H.265 makes the smaller file. H.264 plays on almost anything I plug the drive into.

> **Note:** The codec choice needs storage connected. Without a drive, the button doesn't switch modes. ([Manual FAQ](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/B1MLjXvNAyL.pdf))
{: .note}

From the bitrate table, H.265 is about 25% lower than H.264 at the same Low or High setting. I use H.264 when the file has to play on an older TV or editor.

## PASS-THROUGH AND LATENCY {#pass-through}

On my set I didn't notice lag between the source and the TV. The published spec is under 90 ms (product page) or under 100 ms (KB). I didn't measure it.

On my revision, a 4K60 input passes through and records at 1080p60. A 4K30 or 4K24 input can record in 4K. That's the resolution chart in the original manual.

> **Tip:** For a 4K movie, set the player to 4K24 or auto frame rate so the file matches the content. The current listing also says to turn HDR and Dolby audio off on the source while recording. That note is written for the current model, so I treat it as a maybe on older units. ([Manual FAQ](https://static.cloner-alliance.com/manual/ca-989up_user_manual.pdf), [product page](https://www.cloner-alliance.com/cloneralliance-uhd-pro/))
{: .tip}

## FILES: FINALIZING, PLAYBACK, AND NAMING {#files-and-naming}

The file finishes writing after you stop the recording. Mine played in QuickTime on a Mac with no conversion. On my unit the names are `REC_001.mp4` and so on, and there is no rename command on the device. The current revision can rename from the remote.

> **Tip:** If you rename a file on a computer, keep the `REC` prefix, and keep `IMG` on snapshots. The original manual says the UHD Pro will not play the file back without that prefix.
{: .tip}

## HEAT {#heat}

On long recordings, the USB storage connector on my unit gets hot. The body of the recorder stays warm. That's my test result. The manual only says the unit may become warm. It doesn't call out the connector.

## THE ON-SCREEN MENU {#on-screen-menu}

The menu has five items: Record settings, Schedule settings, System settings, Video playback, and Photo playback.

## BITRATE AND FILE SIZE {#bitrate-and-file-size}

![Record Settings on the UHD Pro, set to H.264, Low, Always display REC, and a 16GB/3H file size](/images/posts/2026-08-24-cloneralliance-uhd-pro/record-settings.jpg)

These rates are from the original manual, in Mbps. Low and High are the menu settings. Each cell is 4K, then 1080p, then 720p, then SD.

| Setting | H.264 | H.265 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low | 4K 16 / 1080p 12 / 720p 8 / SD 4 | 4K 12 / 1080p 8 / 720p 6 / SD 4 |
| High | 4K 20 / 1080p 16 / 720p 12 / SD 8 | 4K 16 / 1080p 12 / 720p 8 / SD 4 |

The file-size options are 16GB/3H, 4GB/3H, and 2GB/3H. Each one is the maximum for a single file. Recording continues in a new file. MP4-Cloner on Windows can join them, and so can any editor.

> **Tip:** FAT32 caps a file at 4 GB. exFAT and NTFS split at about 16.7 GB, or at 3 hours. Use exFAT for long recordings. Drives over 2 TB need exFAT, or they need to be partitioned. ([Manual FAQ](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/B1MLjXvNAyL.pdf))
{: .tip}

The Record Settings screen also has Always display REC, a time watermark, and loop recording. I didn't cover those on camera.

> **Tip:** Turn Always display REC off if the REC badge on the TV bothers you. The manual describes it as a TV-output option. I have not checked whether that badge is burned into the file, so I won't claim that it is.
{: .tip}

> **Tip:** Loop recording ON overwrites the oldest files when the drive fills. OFF stops recording when the drive is full. ([Manual](https://static.cloner-alliance.com/manual/ca-989up_user_manual.pdf))
{: .tip}

## SCHEDULED RECORDING AND THE CLOCK {#scheduled-recording}

My unit can record Once or Every Day, with up to three time ranges. There is no network connection, so the clock does not set itself. Set System time by hand before you make a schedule. The current revision adds a Time Zone setting and allows five ranges.

> **Tip:** Set the clock before you make a schedule. A schedule built on the default clock will fire at the wrong time. ([Manual](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/B1MLjXvNAyL.pdf))
{: .tip}

## SNAPSHOTS AND PHOTO PLAYBACK {#snapshots}

The Snapshot button saves a JPG. You view it on the TV under Photo playback.

## PC CAPTURE MODE: OBS STREAMING TEST {#pc-capture-obs}

The micro-USB port marked "To PC" shows up on macOS as a UVC/UAC device named "CA-989UP UVC". I didn't install a driver. In OBS, add a Video Capture Device and pick CA-989UP UVC. On my revision that input is 1920x1080 at 60 fps. I streamed a Digibox D6 Max through it. The on-screen menu does not appear over USB.

![OBS Studio on a Mac capturing a Digibox through the UHD Pro](/images/posts/2026-08-24-cloneralliance-uhd-pro/obs-capture.jpg)

The KB steps, shortened:

1. Connect the "To PC" cable to the computer.
2. Open OBS and add a Video Capture Device source.
3. Choose CA-989UP UVC.
4. Set the resolution. On my revision, 1920x1080 at 60 fps is the capture mode.

> **Tip:** The on-screen display stays on the TV. It does not show up in the USB picture. ([Manual](https://static.cloner-alliance.com/manual/ca-989up_user_manual.pdf))
{: .tip}

On a USB-C-only Mac, a USB-C to Micro USB adapter handles my unit's micro-USB "To PC" port. The link is in [Accessories](#accessories). The current revision's port is USB-C already, so that adapter is for the original unit.

## TROUBLESHOOTING {#troubleshooting}

- **Black screen, "Unsupported content," or an error message.** The source is sending HDCP-protected video. ClonerAlliance products don't capture that. The manual and the official blog describe this as expected, not a defect. ([Official blog](https://www.cloner-alliance.com/blog/post/cloneralliance-standalone-video-recorders-support-external-media-sticks/))
- **Smart TV apps won't record.** A TV is a display, not a source. Use an external stick or box. Antenna and smart-TV apps are listed as unsupported sources. ([Product page](https://www.cloner-alliance.com/cloneralliance-uhd-pro/), [official blog](https://www.cloner-alliance.com/blog/post/cloneralliance-standalone-video-recorders-support-external-media-sticks/))
- **The top LED blinks blue and green.** There is no input, or the input resolution isn't supported. Check the source output against the resolution chart. ([Manual](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/B1MLjXvNAyL.pdf))
- **The side LED is off, or the drive isn't detected.** The unit doesn't see storage. Plug the drive in before power-on and reboot. ([Manual FAQ](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/B1MLjXvNAyL.pdf))
- **The recording splits into several files.** That's the file-size limit: 4 GB on FAT32, about 16.7 GB or 3 hours on exFAT and NTFS. Join the parts with MP4-Cloner on Windows, or with any editor. ([Manual FAQ](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/B1MLjXvNAyL.pdf))
- **Judder on 4K movies.** The source frame rate doesn't match the content. Set the player to auto frame rate or 4K24. ([Manual FAQ](https://static.cloner-alliance.com/manual/ca-989up_user_manual.pdf))
- **The device doesn't appear in OBS or Device Manager.** Reconnect the USB cable and look for "CA-989UP UAC" or "CA-989UP UVC". ([Manual](https://static.cloner-alliance.com/manual/ca-989up_user_manual.pdf))
